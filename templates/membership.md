### 6\. Duration\, Termination\, and Cost of Membership

**(a) Membership Term and Cancellation.** The membership begins on the Membership Start Date and requires a minimum commitment equal to the Membership Term listed above. After that period, it will automatically continue on a month-to-month basis unless canceled by either FLYING SOLO or the Brand’s Representative with at least 30 days’ written notice.

If FLYING SOLO terminates the Agreement, any unused fees already paid by the Brand’s Representative will be refunded.

To cancel after the initial term, the Brand’s Representative must send a written cancellation notice to designer@flyingsolo.nyc no later than the “Cancellation Notice Deadline” listed in Section (1) Services and Terms.

**(b) Membership Fee.** The Brand’s Representative agrees to pay the membership fee listed above. The first payment is due at the time of signing this Agreement, and the same amount will be billed each month for the duration of the membership. All payments must be made before the 1st day of each month. The Agreement becomes effective upon receipt of the first payment.

**(c) Full-Month Policy.** FLYING SOLO permits full-month memberships only. No pro-rated fees or partial-month memberships are allowed.

**(d) Late Payments.** If the Brand’s Representative fails to pay the monthly membership fee within 30 days of the due date, FLYING SOLO reserves the right to withhold any pending sales payouts until the overdue balance is paid in full. Failure to pay for an entire month is considered a breach of this Agreement, and payouts for that month will not be processed.

### 7\. Refund of Membership Fee\.

Membership fee payments are non-refundable, except in cases where FLYING SOLO has breached this Agreement. FLYING SOLO also reserves the right to terminate the Agreement at any time. If FLYING SOLO terminates the Agreement, a proportional refund will be issued to the Brand’s Representative for any unused portion of the membership fees.

### 8\. Termination Upon Breach\.

If the Brand’s Representative breaches this Agreement, FLYING SOLO has the right to immediately terminate the Agreement and its obligations to the Brand’s Representative. In such a case, both the Brand’s Representative and FLYING SOLO must settle any amounts owed to one another as of the termination date.

### 10\. Stock\.

### Shipping

The Brand is solely responsible for all shipping and delivery costs to the Flying Solo store, as well as any applicable customs duties, taxes, or import fees associated with such delivery.

### Retail Items

FLYING SOLO will track all inventory and provide the Brand’s Representative with sales reports through the Digital Platform. Any discrepancies between stock and sales records must be reported immediately.

If a retail item is lost or damaged, FLYING SOLO will reimburse the Brand’s Representative for the manufacturing or repair cost, up to a maximum of $300 per item. If the actual manufacturing cost exceeds $300, the reimbursement will still be limited to $300. The Brand may choose to obtain additional insurance if desired.

### PR Items

PR items may be returned in “worn” condition as a result of PR pulls or photoshoots. FLYING SOLO will not compensate for any wear and tear that occurs during PR use. Only if a PR item gets lost, FLYING SOLO will compensate for the manufacturing cost, up to a maximum of $300 per item. If the actual manufacturing cost exceeds $300, the reimbursement will still be limited to $300.

At the end of the Agreement, FLYING SOLO will pack the remaining stock for return. The Brand’s Representative has 30 days from the date packing is confirmed to either pick up the items or provide a shipping label. If no action is taken within that time, the items will be donated.

### Editorial Deliverables

FLYING SOLO guarantees delivery of a minimum of one (1) editorial publication per each full month of the Brand's participation with FLYING SOLO.

Editorial placements may be distributed unevenly throughout the term. For example, zero (0) editorials delivered in Month One and two (2) editorials delivered in Month Two shall satisfy the requirement of one (1) editorial per month over a two-month period.

The specific publication(s) shall be determined based on editorial approval and each publication's standard editorial processes and discretion.

An editorial shall be deemed to feature the Brand provided that at least one (1) piece from the Brand's collection is included in the published editorial.

The actual publication date of any editorial may occur after the expiration of the contract term. In such case, FLYING SOLO shall remain obligated to provide the Brand with the editorial upon receipt of the final published materials.

The stylist and/or creative director of the photoshoot shall have full creative discretion to style and accessorize the Brand's pieces as they deem appropriate.
